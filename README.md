- 🎭 Different enemy move sets
- 🔥 Increasing difficulty
- 🏆 Multiple completions
- 🌑 Dark / Gothic atmosphere

Interesting mechanic

One of the main mechanics I'm experimenting with is:

Player takes damage
        ↓
Player becomes stronger

Enemy takes damage
        ↓
Enemy becomes stronger

Player heals
        ↓
Enemy also heals
        ↓
Enemy becomes stronger

This creates a risk/reward loop where healing isn't automatically the safest option.
